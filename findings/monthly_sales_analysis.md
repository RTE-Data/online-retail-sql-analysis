### Monthly Sales Performance

Monthly completed-sales activity increased significantly between October and November 2011. **Completed invoices increased from 2,275 to 3,021 (+32.8%)**, while **items sold increased from 595,073 to 752,907 (+26.5%)**.

Monthly revenue increased from **£1.15 million in October to £1.51 million in November (+30.7%)**. However, average revenue per invoice decreased slightly from **£507.68 to £499.67**, while average items per invoice also fell from approximately **262 to 249**.

This suggests that the increase in November revenue was driven primarily by a higher volume of completed transactions rather than customers spending more per invoice. December should not be directly compared with the preceding full months because the dataset only contains transactions through **9 December 2011**.
